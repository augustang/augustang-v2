AI at Squarespace

In my most recent year at Squarespace, I moved from using AI as a shortcut to establishing AI-native design infrastructure for my team. I've created shared agents and skills, and led the process of unlocking rapid, high-fidelity prototyping directly in our production repos.

Combining formal graphic design training with years of pre-AI product work, vibe coding in production and evolving my workflows has led me to author agentic onboarding for a new AI-native product, automate rote cross-functional handoffs, and give my design team a path to production-grade prototyping without engineering dependency.

What started as individual experiments with skills and agents, quickly transformed into reusable team tools. Early 2026, Squarespace released a new internal app hosting platform. Highly useful, but very technical for PMs and designers with no coding experience to navigate.

I created and shared across the company, a set of skills to aid with deploy processes and troubleshooting. Before non-engineer access to our sqsp repositories, I also recreated a component and styles starter kit for anyone needing to vibe code frontend prototypes for the .com.
